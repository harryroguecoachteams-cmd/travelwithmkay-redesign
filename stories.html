Madren

Bootcamp graduate, coached with her son

“I thought it was $25, but Maria challenged… we found that traveling on a Kenyan passport to Vietnam is a bit more complex.”
Came in
A mother planning a US summer trip with her teenage son, who had just started university.
Changed
When the costs came up on screen, World Cup prices made the US a poor fit. They chose Cape Town instead, and her son learned to budget.
Read what Madren said

She doesn't book the travel or the itinerary. She really is just a coach. She's someone who will help you to think through destinations, ask the right questions, and help you arrive at a decision that works for you and for your situation. And we had an example recently when we were talking about Vietnam, and trying to look for the cost of the visa, and I thought it was $25, but Maria challenged, and she was like, no, I don't think so. And this is where her experience then comes in, and she's like, that doesn't sound right, can you please go back and check again? And truly, truly, we found that traveling on a Kenyan passport to Vietnam is a bit more complex, and you need an intermediary to be able to get that sorted for you, and that takes time and the cost can build up. So with her help, we were able to explore other potential destinations, and now we're planning to do a trip to Cape Town next month.
