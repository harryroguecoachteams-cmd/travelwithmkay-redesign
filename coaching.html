What does a travel coach do?

A travel coach helps you plan and book your own trips with confidence. Together we work through your destination, dates, budget, visas, flights and safety, but you make the decisions and you do the booking. The goal is that you can plan the next trip without me.

Is a travel coach the same as a travel agent?

No. A travel agent books a trip for you, usually from packages. I don't book anything and I don't sell packages. I teach you how to do it, with the same tools and checks I have used across 100 countries.

Who do you coach?

Mostly African women over 40 who are done waiting to travel. I also coach families, couples and experienced travelers who want a better system. Clients join from Kenya and the diaspora, including the US.

Can you help with visas on an African passport?

Yes. Visa rules are one of the most common reasons trips stall. I show you how to check current rules from official sources, which countries are visa-free or e-visa for your passport, and how to prepare a strong application. Nobody can honestly guarantee a visa decision, and I won't pretend to.

How do sessions work?

Everything happens online. Choose a call or the bootcamp, pay securely by card or M-Pesa, then pick a time on my calendar. After a call you get a short written summary and a list of resources.

What if I have never traveled abroad?

That's exactly where many of my clients start. My own first trip abroad was a school exchange in 1989. We begin with what you want to feel, choose an easy first destination, and build your confidence one step at a time.
