Is it for you?

Coaching works best when you want the skill, not just the trip.

A good fit if you

Keep postponing trips because of fear, guilt, money or visas. Want to stop depending on agents. Are planning a first solo trip, a family trip or a milestone birthday. Have an itinerary that doesn't feel right yet.

Not the right fit if you

Want someone to book everything for you. In that case a good travel agent is the better choice, and I'm happy to say so.

What you need

A phone or laptop, an internet connection, and the honest answer to one question: where do you want to go?

Questions

Before you book

What does a travel coach do?

A travel coach helps you plan and book your own trips with confidence. We work through your destination, dates, budget, visas, flights and safety together, but you make the decisions and you do the booking. The goal is that you can plan the next trip without me.

Is a travel coach the same as a travel agent?

No. A travel agent books a trip for you, usually from packages. I don't book anything and I don't sell packages. I teach you how to do it, with the same tools and checks I use myself after 100 countries.

Can you help with visas on an African passport?

Yes. Visa rules are one of the most common reasons trips stall. I show you how to check current rules from official sources, which countries are visa-free or e-visa for your passport, and how to prepare a strong application. Nobody can honestly guarantee a visa decision, and I won't pretend to.

How do sessions work?

Everything happens online. Choose a call or the bootcamp, pay securely by card or M-Pesa, then pick a time on my calendar. After a call you get a short written summary and a list of resources.
