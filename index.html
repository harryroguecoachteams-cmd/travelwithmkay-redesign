Coach, not agent

I don't sell trips. I teach you to plan them.

Most people come to me after years of letting someone else decide. Here is the difference.

A travel agent

Books one trip, usually from packages that make business sense for them. Next time, you start again.

AI and Google

Hand you a confident list in seconds. They don't know your passport, your budget or what worries you, and the visa rules they quote are sometimes out of date.

A travel coach

Works on how you decide: destination, dates, money, visas and safety. You book it yourself, and you can do it again without me.

“While an agent would book just one trip for you, a coach leaves you able to book every trip after that.”Maria, on her August 2026 webinar

How coaching works

From “one day” to “I'm going.”

Every trip that actually happens clears the same three checks. We clear them together.

  1. Clarity and courage

    We swap what-ifs for a calm plan: where, when, how much, and what worries you most.

    You leave with your why, a simple route and two next actions.

  2. Plan like a pro

    Visas, flights, timing, money and packing, in the right order, with the tools I use myself.

    You leave with the order of tasks, the key links and the trade-offs that save money.

  3. Travel your way

    Solo or with friends, lean or luxe. We build your safety routine and a plan B.

    You leave with a first-day plan, a backup plan and real confidence.

Questions

What people ask before they book

What does a travel coach do?

A travel coach helps you plan and book your own trips with confidence. We work through your destination, dates, budget, visas, flights and safety together, but you make the decisions and you do the booking. The goal is that you can plan the next trip without me.

Is a travel coach the same as a travel agent?

No. A travel agent books a trip for you, usually from packages. I don't book anything and I don't sell packages. I teach you how to do it, with the same tools and checks I use myself after 100 countries.

Who do you coach?

Mostly African women over 40 who are done waiting to travel. I also coach families, couples and experienced travelers who want a better system. Clients join from Kenya and the diaspora, including the US.

Do I need a big budget?

No. Smart trade-offs beat big budgets. We decide where comfort matters to you, save everywhere else, and start early enough to get better prices.

Can you help with visas on an African passport?

Yes. Visa rules are one of the most common reasons trips stall. I show you how to check current rules from official sources, which countries are visa-free or e-visa for your passport, and how to prepare a strong application. Nobody can honestly guarantee a visa decision, and I won't pretend to.

How do sessions work?

Everything happens online. Choose a call or the bootcamp, pay securely by card or M-Pesa, then pick a time on my calendar. After a call you get a short written summary and a list of resources.

Where are you based?

Nairobi, Kenya. I coach clients online, wherever they are.
