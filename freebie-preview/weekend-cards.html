Before you leave Nairobi

Ten minutes now saves the trip

No cash

KWS park gates do not take cash

Hell's Gate, Mt. Longonot, Ol Donyo Sabuk and Mt. Kenya are paid on eCitizen or KWSPay with M-Pesa. The Forest at Kereita is M-Pesa only. Set up eCitizen and link M-Pesa before the day: it takes about 10 minutes.

  • eCitizen account set up, M-Pesa linked
  • M-Pesa topped up for gates and zip lines
  • Your ID: the fees in this guide are citizen adult rates
  • Small cash for matatus and boda bodas
  • Leave early: most gates open at 6am
  • Check the forecast the day before

Ngong Hills

Nairobi's backyard

FromRailways Stage
ToNgong Hills
Matatu 111 to Ngong Town, then a boda to the gate
At the gateKSh 200
TotalKSh1,300all in, per person
Best months
JanFebMarAprMayJunJulAugSepOctNovDec

Avoid it after heavy rain: the trail turns slippery and you lose the views.

Getting there

Matatu Route 111 from Railways Stage (opposite Memorial Park) to Ngong Town, KSh 50 to 100. Boda boda to the Forest Reserve gate, KSh 100.

Entry and how to pay

KSh 200 (citizen adult). The hike only needs the entry ticket. Zip line KSh 700, bike hire KSh 500 for 3 hours.

What to carry

A warm layer (it's cold up there even in the dry season), snacks and water: there's nothing on the trail.

Sort before you leave
  • Your entry ticket, the afternoon before. It gets you the 6am gate opening instead of 8am.
Pay on the day
  • Matatu, KSh 50 to 100
  • Boda to the gate, KSh 100

Lake Naivasha & Hell's Gate

Cycle among zebras

FromNyamakima, Odeon or Accra Road
ToLake Naivasha & Hell's Gate
Matatu to Naivasha (1.5 to 2 hrs), then the 'Kongoni' matatu
At the gateKSh 500 + 300 cycling
TotalKSh4,300to 5,400 per person
Best months
JanFebMarAprMayJunJulAugSepOctNovDec

The gorge walk may close in heavy rains (March to May).

Getting there

Matatu from Nyamakima, Odeon or Accra Road, KSh 300 to 500 one way (1.5 to 2 hours). Then the 'Kongoni' matatu to Hell's Gate, KSh 100 to 150.

Entry and how to pay Cashless gate

Hell's Gate KSh 500 (citizen, KWS scenic park) + cycling fee KSh 300. Bike hire at Elsa Gate KSh 400 to 600: bargain a little.

What to carry

Sunscreen and water for the cycle, a warm layer for the night by the lake, your own tent if camping.

Sort before you leave
  • Hell's Gate entry on eCitizen. The park is cashless.
Pay on the day
  • Matatus, KSh 300 to 500 + 100 to 150
  • Bike hire at Elsa Gate, KSh 400 to 600
  • Fisherman's Camp, KSh 900 with your own tent

Weekend 03 · 50 to 65 km from Nairobi · Day or one night

Thika & Fourteen Falls

A waterfall 45 minutes away

FromOdeon or Ronald Ngala Street
ToThika & Fourteen Falls
Matatu 237 to Thika, then towards Kilimambogo + boda
At the gateKSh 200
TotalKSh3,450to 5,450 with a night
Best water
JanFebMarAprMayJunJulAugSepOctNovDec

Most impressive during or just after the rains. The dry season gives clearer access to the riverbank.

Getting there

Matatu Route 237 from Odeon or Ronald Ngala Street, KSh 150 to 300. From Thika, matatu from Makongeni Stage towards Kilimambogo, then boda to the falls.

Entry and how to pay

Fourteen Falls KSh 200 (citizens). Blue Post Hotel nature park KSh 150 per adult: crocodiles, ostriches, colobus monkeys.

What to carry

Shoes with grip for the wet rocks, a plastic bag for your phone near the spray.

Sort before you leave
  • A guesthouse in Thika or Juja (KSh 1,000 to 2,500) if you stay over
Pay on the day
  • Fourteen Falls and Blue Post entries
  • Matatus and boda

Machakos

Free park entry, an hour away

FromMachakos Country Bus Station, Muthurwa
ToMachakos
Matatu (very frequent), then a boda or tuk-tuk
At the gateFree
TotalKSh3,450to 5,850 with a night
All year
JanFebMarAprMayJunJulAugSepOctNovDec

Semi-arid: it stays warm and dry when Nairobi is having one of its grey rainy stretches.

Getting there

Matatu from Machakos Country Bus Station near Muthurwa, KSh 200 to 400. Very frequent. Boda or tuk-tuk to People's Park, KSh 100 to 200.

Entry and how to pay

People's Park and Iveti Hills hiking: FREE. Zip line inside the park KSh 550 (two zips).

What to carry

Sun hat and water, snacks for the children, comfortable shoes for the park paths.

Sort before you leave
  • Machakos Inn, Garden Hotel (KSh 1,500 to 2,500) or Kyaka Hotel, if staying over
Pay on the day
  • Everything else. Park entry is free.
  • Zip line, KSh 550 for two zips

Weekend 05 · 90 km from Nairobi · Day trip

Mt. Longonot

Hike a volcano

FromNyamakima or Accra Road
ToMt. Longonot
Naivasha matatu, then a boda to the park gate
At the gateKSh 500, cashless
TotalKSh2,100to 2,800 per person
No set season in my guide. What matters is the hour.

Timing: Start by 7am to beat the heat and the afternoon clouds. Rim in 1.5 to 2 hours, full crater circuit another 2 to 3. Gates 6am to 6pm.

Getting there

Same route as Naivasha (Nyamakima or Accra Road, KSh 300 to 500), then boda to the park gate, KSh 150 to 300.

Entry and how to pay Cashless gate

KSh 500 (citizen, KWS scenic park). Pay on eCitizen with M-Pesa BEFORE you arrive. The gate does not take cash.

What to carry

2+ litres of water (nothing on the trail), proper shoes for the loose-rock descent, a packed lunch.

Sort before you leave
  • Entry on eCitizen with M-Pesa. The gate takes no cash.
Pay on the day
  • Matatu, KSh 300 to 500
  • Boda to the gate, KSh 150 to 300

Limuru & Kereita Forest

East Africa's longest zip line

FromKhoja Stage, near Kencom
ToLimuru & Kereita Forest
Matatu 114 to Limuru, towards Kimende, then a boda
At the gateKSh 200 + zip, M-Pesa only
TotalKSh3,300to 3,900 per person
No set season in my guide. What matters is the hour.

Timing: Go before 10am, when the mist sits in the tree canopy. Midweek is quieter.

Getting there

Matatu Route 114 from Khoja Stage near Kencom to Limuru, KSh 80 to 200. Matatu towards Kimende, then boda to The Forest gate, KSh 200 to 400.

Entry and how to pay M-Pesa only

Forest entry KSh 200. 2-line zip (750 m) KSh 1,800 to 2,100, 6-line zip (2.2 km) KSh 2,800 to 3,250. M-Pesa only, no cash.

What to carry

A warm layer for the highland mist, closed shoes for the zip line, a full M-Pesa balance.

Sort before you leave
  • The Kentmere Club in Tigoni (B&B from KSh 2,000 per person), if staying
  • Top up M-Pesa: the gate and zips take nothing else
Pay on the day
  • Entry and zips, by M-Pesa
  • Matatus and boda

Nanyuki & Mt. Kenya

Ride the KSh 400 train

FromSafari train, Fridays 9am
ToNanyuki & Mt. Kenya
Arrives 4:49pm. Back Sunday 9am, arrives 4:46pm
At the gateKSh 800 / 24 hrs
TotalKSh3,400to 5,000 per person
Best months
JanFebMarAprMayJunJulAugSepOctNovDec

Cloud rolls in by midday, so be on the trail before 9am.

Getting there

Safari train every Friday 9am, arrives 4:49pm; back Sunday 9am, arrives 4:46pm. KSh 400 economy, KSh 1,000 first class. Or matatu (4NTE, Chakana) KSh 700 to 1,000.

Entry and how to pay Cashless gate

Mt. Kenya National Park KSh 800 per 24 hours (citizen). Public campsite KSh 200 a night. All fees on KWSPay (cashless).

What to carry

A warm, waterproof layer even in July: altitude and Kenyan weather are unpredictable together.

Sort before you leave
  • Call Kenya Railways on 0709 907 000 to confirm the train and how to buy. It is NOT on the SGR booking site.
  • Park and campsite fees on KWSPay
Pay on the day
  • Matatu instead of the train (4NTE, Chakana), KSh 700 to 1,000

Weekend 09 · 95 km from Nairobi · One night

Sagana

Kenya's only white water rafting

FromTea Room or Accra Road
ToSagana
Nyeri-bound matatu, alight at Sagana (1.5 to 2 hrs)
At the gateRafting from KSh 5,500
TotalKSh7,900to 10,000 per person
Best water
JanFebMarAprMayJunJulAugSepOctNovDec

Dry season rapids are calmer but still fun.

Getting there

Nyeri-bound matatu from Tea Room or Accra Road, alight at Sagana, KSh 300 to 500 (1.5 to 2 hours). A shared car is easier if you can split fuel.

Entry and how to pay

Short rafting (1 to 2 hours) about KSh 5,500, long rafting KSh 7,750, bungee KSh 3,000 to 5,000. Camping KSh 1,000 to 2,000.

What to carry

Clothes you can get wet, a change of clothes, sandals that strap on, sunscreen. Self-catering supplies from Nairobi save the most.

Sort before you leave
  • Call the camps directly (Rapids Camp, Savage Wilderness): direct rates are often better than online
  • Groups of 4+: ask for a package
Pay on the day
  • Matatu, KSh 300 to 500

Weekend 10 · 65 km from Nairobi · Day trip

Ol Donyo Sabuk

The hidden gem

FromOdeon or Ronald Ngala Street
ToOl Donyo Sabuk
Matatu 237 to Thika, towards Kilimambogo, then a boda
At the gateKSh 500, cashless
TotalKSh1,700to 2,000 per person
No set season in my guide. What matters is the hour.

Timing: Start before 9am: the summit hike is about 5 hours return. On a clear day you see Mt. Kenya and Kilimanjaro.

Getting there

Thika-bound matatu (Route 237, KSh 150 to 300), then a matatu towards Kilimambogo (KSh 100 to 200) and a boda to the park gate (KSh 100 to 200).

Entry and how to pay Cashless gate

KSh 500 (citizen, KWS scenic park). eCitizen and M-Pesa only. A KWS ranger guide goes with every hiker (buffalo are common).

What to carry

2+ litres of water, a rain jacket whatever the season (the upper forest is dense and wet), a packed lunch.

Sort before you leave
  • Entry on eCitizen. The gate takes no cash.
Pay on the day
  • Matatus and boda, about KSh 350 to 700
  • Your KWS ranger guide walks with you

Packing

What goes in the bag

One list for every weekend, plus what to add when it's raining or when you're staying the night.

Every weekend

The basics

  • A warm layer: Ngong, Limuru and Nanyuki are cold even in the dry season
  • A rain jacket, whatever the season
  • 2 litres of water per person for hikes: there is nothing on the trails
  • Proper shoes: Longonot's descent is loose rock
  • Packed lunch and snacks
  • Phone charged, eCitizen open, M-Pesa topped up
  • ID for citizen rates
March to May, October to November

Rainy months

  • Shoes with grip, and a spare pair of socks
  • A dry bag or plastic bag for phone and ID
  • A plan B: Machakos or Thika instead of a muddy hike
  • Check that Hell's Gate gorge is open
Staying the night

Overnight

  • Your own tent if camping (Fisherman's Camp, Ngong, Mt. Kenya)
  • Headtorch and a warm sleeping layer
Going soon?

Best in November and December

  • Fourteen Falls (page 6) is at its most impressive just after the rains.
  • Sagana (page 12) has its best rafting water in November and December.
  • Machakos (page 7) stays warm and dry when Nairobi is grey and rainy.
