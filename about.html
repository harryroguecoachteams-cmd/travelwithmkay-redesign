About Maria

Thirty-seven years, 100 countries, and a lot of planning.

No lottery win, no rich uncle, no sponsor. Just planning, done over and over. Planning is the part I can teach you.

The short version

Country 001 to country 100

I was born and raised in Kenya, where coastal buses, overnight trains and road trips were my first classrooms. I've since explored all 47 Kenyan counties.

  1. 1989 · Country 001

    Germany, on a school trip

    An ordinary school trip with my classmates. It lit something in me that never went out.

  2. 2012

    Summit of Kilimanjaro

    One slow breath, one careful step, and a summit I still carry with me.

  3. Along the way

    Becoming a coach

    I trained as an ICF-certified coach so I could teach what I had learned the hard way, and started Travel with Mkay.

  4. 2026 · Country 100

    São Tomé and Príncipe

    Thirty-seven years and five continents after that school trip, the hundredth stamp. Thirty-nine of the 100 are in Africa.

Why I coach

If I'd known then what I know now

I would have saved hundreds, sometimes thousands, of dollars, traveled more days for the same money, and done it with far less stress.

That hindsight is why I coach. Agents can book a trip and AI can write a list, but neither will sit with your fears, check your passport realities, or notice when you are overwhelmed. That is the work I do.
